Unfortunately this is not entirely an easy question to answer, since the market is extremely fragmented. Let's make an overview of what's available via a service that I already know that exists and what it potentially offers to its users. There are a few areas that I already identified as that could be lacking, nevertheless let's dissect this and see exactly for what it is.

Hold up.

At this point I see certain issues attached.

First of all, why would I share such sensitive data as my calendar or email details? I know this is a startup, however providing access to such third party companies just does not feel entirely safe.

Secondly, I see a range of data that the website claims to monitor, however it is quite unlikely for it to keep track of. For instance body fat, which may only be tracked by the use of specialistic medical devices. Many details such as the monitoring of alcoholic drinks, fibre, sodium, sugar, cholesterol is unfeasible on top of which finances cannot be tracked at all at present time. There is however one area that I feel might be especially disregarded, which is the media.

Where are video games? Where are TV Shows, movies? How about books or audiobooks? Perhaps comics, manga, anime? Radio shows? There are so many tools that do empower the user to keep track of their media consumption and I believe it influences people on a substantial level.

How about tracking the learning progress of languages? Still, that having said, it seems the application does cover a quite substantial area and I can't wait to trying it out.

One wild card which I see is RescueTime which I will look into at a later stage.
